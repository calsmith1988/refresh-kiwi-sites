1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help websites remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies we use

Analytics cookies

  • Name: _ga, _gid, etc.
  • Purpose: Distinguish users and measure site usage.
  • Provider: Google Analytics.
  • Duration: Up to 2 years.

Embedded content cookies

Set by third parties (e.g. YouTube, Google Maps) when you view or interact with embedded videos or maps. For details, see the relevant third party's cookie policy.

3. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. To manage cookies:

  • Visit your browser's settings or preferences.
  • For analytics opt-out, see tools such as Google's opt-out browser add-on.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy. Check the "Last updated" date at the top of this page for the current version.
