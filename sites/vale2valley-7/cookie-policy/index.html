1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit websites. They help remember your preferences and enable various website functions.

2. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

Session cookies that enable core site functionality (e.g. security, network management).

Analytics cookies

Used to collect information about how visitors use our site (e.g. Google Analytics _ga, _gid).

3. Third-party cookies

Embedded content (maps, videos) may set cookies from their own domains. We do not control these cookies and recommend checking the third party's privacy or cookie policy for details.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies via your browser settings. Note that blocking certain cookies may affect site performance.
