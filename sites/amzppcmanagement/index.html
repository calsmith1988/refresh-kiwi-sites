FAQ

Hiring the right Amazon ads agency will boost your growth

Browse through our FAQs to see if we're the right fit for you.

What qualifies a brand to work with you?

We partner with ambitious eCommerce brands that are ready to scale on Amazon — typically brands with proven products, healthy margins, and a commitment to long-term growth across one or more marketplaces.

Do you provide a free Amazon advertising audit?

Yes. Our strategy call includes a review of your account's current performance and an outline of the hidden opportunities we'd pursue first.

What is your fee structure?

Fees are structured around your ad spend and scope of work. We'll walk through the details transparently on your strategy call — no surprises.

What are your contract terms?

We believe in earning your business through results. Contract terms are discussed upfront during onboarding so you know exactly what to expect.

You'll have direct access to the team managing your account — regular updates, clear reporting, and responsive communication when you need it.

How do you set up account access?

We guide you through Amazon's secure authorization process so we can manage campaigns without disrupting your existing setup.

How quickly can you begin?

After your strategy call and onboarding, we can typically begin managing campaigns within days — depending on account complexity and marketplace access.
