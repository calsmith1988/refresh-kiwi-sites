FAQ

Questions patients ask most often

Unsure about something? You're not the only one — here are clear answers to help you feel confident before your visit.

Is there parking nearby the clinic?

Yes! There is lots of convenient street parking near the clinic.

How much does treatment cost?

An initial appointment lasts 60 minutes and costs £50. A follow-up appointment lasts 45 minutes and costs £45.

See our services page for full details.

What should I wear to my appointments?

Your normal clothes, but you can bring shorts and a vest to change into.

What if I do not feel comfortable with certain techniques?

I will not use any technique you do not like — we can find another way.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you're running late, please let me know ASAP and I will see what I can do according to the day's schedule. If you cannot make it, please let me know ASAP so the slot can be reallocated.

Is osteopathy safe?

Osteopathic treatment is very safe and a natural approach to healthcare. I work with my patients and tailor treatments specifically to their bodies' needs.

Osteopathy is a recognised health profession, and has the same safeguards that you would enjoy if you visited a doctor or a dentist.

How long will I need to wait for an appointment?

I can often offer same-day appointments even if there are no available slots on my online booking system. Phone me to check availability for that day — I will always do what I can to see you as soon as possible.

Are there toilets available at the clinic?

Yes! There are toilets at the clinic for you to use.

What if I am late or cannot make my appointment?

Please let me know ASAP if you need to cancel your appointment, with a minimum of 24 hours notice.

Still have a question?

Call or email — I'm happy to help before you book.
