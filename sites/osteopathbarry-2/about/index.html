About the clinic

Whole-life care in the heart of Barry

A welcoming clinic dedicated to personalised osteopathy for families across Barry and the Vale of Glamorgan.

Welcome

Your health journey starts here

Located in the heart of Barry and ideal for patient comfort, the clinic is dedicated to whole-life, personalised care. I aim to reduce your pain and improve your wellness by finding the root cause of your discomfort and empowering you on your health journey.

From detailed assessment to hands-on treatment and practical advice, every visit is tailored to you — not a template.

Meet Jolyon

Welsh Osteopath of the Year

I received my Master's in Osteopathy at Swansea University with First-Class Honours. I am a passionate, hard-working osteopath, and I care about and for all my patients' physical and mental wellbeing.

I have additional qualifications in soft tissue therapy and meditation, and I am looking forward to attaining further qualifications in cranial osteopathy and equine and canine osteopathy. I treat patients with a variety of conditions including headaches, muscle aches and chronic pain.

When you come to see me, I will work with you and adjust all your treatment to your individual needs. In my free time, I enjoy reading and being outside with my horses and dogs.
