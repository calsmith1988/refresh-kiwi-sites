FAQ

Hiring the right Amazon ads agency

Browse through our FAQs to see if we're the right fit for you.

What qualifies a brand to work with you?

We partner with brands investing a minimum of $1,500 / £1,500 per month in Amazon advertising. This ensures we can deliver meaningful results and ROI for your business.

Do you provide a free Amazon advertising audit?

Yes, we offer comprehensive ad account audits at no cost. We recommend starting with a brief introductory call to understand your goals and ensure we're the right fit for your brand.

What is your fee structure?

Our pricing model consists of a flat monthly retainer per marketplace region (e.g., North America, United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, India) plus a performance-based fee tied to sales growth above your previous 3-month average, aligned with agreed-upon TACoS/TRoAS targets.

What are your contract terms?

We operate on monthly rolling contracts to maintain flexibility for our clients. However, we're proud that many of our client relationships span 5+ years — a testament to the consistent results we deliver.

How do you handle client communication?

We maintain strong communication through weekly scheduled strategy calls, detailed Loom video analyses for in-depth insights, and regular updates via Slack, WhatsApp, or email based on your preference.

How quickly can you begin?

We can initiate our partnership immediately following our initial call and audit. Our streamlined onboarding process allows us to begin optimizing your campaigns right away.
